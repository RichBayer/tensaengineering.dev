Controlled AI systems for real environments.

TENSA Engineering is the public home for NeuroCore, Argus ACLI, and Argus Lab — a growing ecosystem focused on local-first AI, grounded system intelligence, controlled interaction, and real Linux troubleshooting.

The idea

AI can reason, but authority must be governed.

TENSA Engineering is focused on building AI systems that understand real environments without giving intelligence uncontrolled authority. The goal is simple: less guessing, more signal, and AI output grounded in real system state.
