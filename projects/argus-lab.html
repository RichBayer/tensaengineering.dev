Argus Lab

Real Linux troubleshooting practice and diagnostic validation.

Argus Lab is planned as a real-Linux training and validation environment built around controlled failures, resettable scenarios, mentor-style AI guidance, and measurable troubleshooting progress.

The important idea is simple: real skill comes from solving real problems.

Training

Users work through realistic Linux failures instead of only memorizing commands or following clean tutorials.

Validation

Known failure scenarios can test whether Argus identifies symptoms, severity, recommendations, and evidence correctly.

Progression

Long term, scenarios can become harder as users improve and may support tracked troubleshooting growth.
